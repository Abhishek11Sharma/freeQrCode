QR & Barcode Scan: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Last updated: 17 November 2026

QR & Barcode Scan ("the app"), published under the name FreeQRCode by Abhishek Sharma ("we", "us"), lets you create, scan and manage QR codes and barcodes. This page explains what the app does with your information. Questions: tost9870@gmail.com.

In short: the app works entirely on your device. There is no account, no server and no upload. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. On Android the app does not even hold the Internet permission.

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no backend, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We never receive the codes you scan or create, your history, or anything about how you use the app.

2. Camera

  • The camera is used only to scan QR codes and barcodes, and only while the Scan screen is open. It is released when you leave it.
  • Camera frames are analysed on your device by an on-device barcode detector. Images are not saved, recorded or uploaded.
  • The camera is optional. Creating, styling and exporting codes work without it, and you can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

3. Photos and files

  • When you choose a picture to scan a code from, or to use as a logo, the app reads only the picture you pick. It stays on your device.
  • When you export (PNG, SVG, PDF, ZIP, HTML, CSV or vCard), the file is written where you choose through the system save dialog, photo library or share sheet. The app requests no general storage permission.

4. Data stored on your device

To make the app useful, the following is kept in the app's private storage on your phone only:

  • QR codes and barcodes you create, your designs, templates and favourites;
  • scan history (you can turn this off, and choose whether sensitive scans such as Wi-Fi passwords are saved);
  • optional local usage statistics, shown only to you and switchable off in Settings;
  • your settings.

You can delete any item, clear your history, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. We have no copy.

5. Backups

The optional backup feature creates a file that is encrypted on your device with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key). You decide where that file goes. We cannot read it and cannot recover a lost password.

6. Scanned content and links

  • Decoding a code happens on your device and its content is never sent to us.
  • If a code contains a link, phone number, email, UPI payment, map location or similar, the app only opens it when you tap to do so (or if you turn on "auto-open links" in Settings). It is then handed to another app, such as your browser, dialer or payment app, and that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Please check links before opening them. The app warns about content it considers unsafe, but cannot guarantee that a third-party website is safe.

7. Notifications and vibration

Optional local notifications (for example, a bulk job finished or a backup reminder) are created on your device. There is no push service, and notifications stay off until you turn them on in Settings. Vibration is used only for scan feedback.

8. Third-party services

  • On-device barcode detection: scanning uses Google's ML Kit library running locally. On Android the app removes the Internet and network-state permissions, so this library cannot send data from the app.
  • App updates (Google Play only): the app may ask the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version is available. Nothing about your codes or usage is shared. Google's handling of Play Store data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • App stores: Google Play or the Apple App Store may collect information when you download the app, under their own policies.

We use no advertising networks, analytics SDKs or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

9. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself. Keeping your phone locked and up to date helps protect what is stored on it.

11. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything the app stores is under your control on your device. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and delete them on request.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
